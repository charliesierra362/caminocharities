Service to our community · Oklahoma

One clay at a time, we're breaking hunger in Oklahoma.

Food insecurity still touches too many Oklahoma families — especially in rural and low-income communities. Camino Charities, the giving arm of Camino Natural Resources, turns community engagement into millions of meals through events like the annual Shell Out for Hunger Clay Shoot.

MILE 1 WHY WE SERVE

Hunger doesn't follow section lines.

Food disparities contribute to poor health outcomes, educational setbacks, and economic instability across the communities where we live and operate. Camino answers with strategy and shoulder-to-shoulder work — combining community engagement with focused fundraising to provide immediate relief and build long-term resilience.

WHEREAS, no family's table should sit empty in a state this generous;

AND WHEREAS, immediate relief without long-term resilience is only half the job;

NOW THEREFORE, we commit our hands, hours, and dollars to the fight against food insecurity. Nourish. Heal. Serve.
MILE 2 HOW WE WORK

Three words. One promise.

Nourish Meals on tables

Dollars raised become millions of meals through the Oklahoma Food Bank, reaching the rural and low-income communities where access to nutritious food is hardest to come by.

Heal Families on their feet

Partnering with Youth and Family Services and ICAN to give families in crisis the resources to rebuild — because recovery takes more than a single meal.

Serve Neighbors beside neighbors

Camino employees show up in person — organizing, volunteering, and fundraising — turning a company footprint into a community handprint.
